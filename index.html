2. EXPANSION

Emerald Gate

Dungeon Vhalorium™ - Emerald Gate is a massive expansion on the original dark fantasy adventure, offering players a deeper, more dangerous world to explore. This installment focuses on non-linear progression, enhanced crafting systems, and the looming threat of the Obsidian Heart, a force threatening to corrupt the entire Vhalorium complex. Expanded World: The total playable area is 150% larger than the first Dungeon Vhalorium, featuring three new biomes: the Crystalline Depths, the Forgotten Clockwork, and the Ashen Catacombs. Weapon Fusion & Runes: Introducing a robust Runecrafting System. Players can now fuse two existing weapons to create powerful Legendary variants and socket them with rare runes, providing unique, build-defining bonuses. The primary antagonist is not a single boss, but the Obsidian Heart itself—a spreading corruption that dynamically changes the layout and enemy types of explored areas, ensuring no two playthroughs are exactly alike. Face new strategic challenges with the introduction of Shadow Weavers (ranged casters) and Goliath Constructs (slow, heavily armored tanks requiring environmental manipulation to defeat). Entry Paths: Seamless Progression

4. SANCTUARY

Blossom Path

Dungeon Vhalorium: Blossom Path is the peaceful heart of the Vhalorium universe a "Place of Memories" where the battle stops and the story begins. Unlike the dark corridors of Ghost Villa, the Blossom Path is a sanctuary filled with cherry blossoms, emotional figures, and hidden lore. It serves as the ultimate Bridge, containing ancient portals that allow you to teleport seamlessly between every world in the Dungeon Vhalorium series. The Bridge: A central hub connecting all current and future Vhalorium games. The Sanctuary: A strictly "No-Battle" zone designed for peaceful exploration and reflection. The Memories: Discover emotional scenes and statues that reveal the deeper history of the world. Experience a world of harmony, light, and beauty before stepping back into the shadows.

7. LOGIC RUNNER

Tactical Run

Welcome to a brutal new challenge in the Dungeon Vhalorium™ universe. This isn't just another brainless runner...this is a high-stakes tactical mind game built by Cytronyx to completely break your focus. The rules seem simple on paper, but the environment is working entirely against you. A relentless day and night cycle flips every single minute, completely resetting your visibility and forcing you to readapt on the fly. To make things worse, aggressive enemies and tricky teleportation loops are specifically placed to disorient you and trap you in endless puzzles. You will need serious logic, environmental analysis, and split-second decisions to break the loop and escape. Look closely at the layout, time your runs, and don't panic. One wrong step, and it's over.

This game is brutal, and it is very difficult for people to play. It is more than just a game. The game takes place in a necro-world built by Dungeon Vhalorium, where people who have committed sins and terrible crimes are punished by the Gate Entity. You are entering and watching one of Dungeon Vhalorium’s sinister and awful reality worlds, where no mercy will be given. You are inside a world built by the Entity for the punishment of your crimes. This world has no escape; all you can do is run. The Necro will hunt you and tear your soul, spirit, and body apart. You are immortal here; you will face punishments worse than death. No human soul can handle this, yet the Lord of this world heals your soul so you will go through every awful punishment until the Lord is satisfied. In the worst case, the Lord can even control time. This world is his creation, and he can do anything here. The Lord created this world to punish evil demons, gods, and celestial beings.
