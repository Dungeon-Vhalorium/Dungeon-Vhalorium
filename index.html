A World With No Escape

This game is brutal, and it is very difficult for people to play. It is more than just a game. The game takes place in a necro-world built by Dungeon Vhalorium, where people who have committed sins and terrible crimes are punished by the Terrible Entity.

You are inside a world built by the Entity for the punishment of your crimes. This world has no escape, all you can do is run. The Necro will hunt you and tear your soul, spirit, and body apart.

No Mercy:

You are entering one of Dungeon Vhalorium's sinister and awful reality worlds, where no mercy will be given.

Worse Than Death:

You are immortal here; you will face punishments worse than death. No human soul can handle this, yet the Lord of this world heals your soul so you will go through every awful punishment until the Lord is satisfied.

Lord Controls Time:

In the worst case, the Lord can even control time. This world is his creation, and he can do anything here. The Lord created this world to punish evil demons, gods, and celestial beings.

Dungeon Vhalorium™ - The Crimson Rift

The Grave of a Kingdom

The world has been reduced to soot. In this new beginning for the Dungeon Vhalorium series, players are dropped into the heart of a mystery that has wiped out an entire civilization. The Crimson Rift is not just a dungeon—it is the grave of a kingdom, where magma flows through ruins and the air is heavy with the weight of the past.

A New Narrative Start:

Begin the journey in a world where life has been extinguished. You are the only living presence left to witness the aftermath of a catastrophic event that has yet to be revealed.

The Scorched Realm:

Explore a "High-Intensity" environment featuring obsidian architecture, rivers of molten magma, and a sky filled with permanent ash.

Infernal Guardians:

Face a hierarchy of fire-based entities. From magma-infused monsters to ancient dragons, every step toward the heart of the rift is a fight for survival.

The Crimson Boss:

Encounter the powerful entity responsible for guarding the rift. Defeat this massive guardian to stop the corruption from spreading and begin uncovering the truth of what happened!

Dungeon Vhalorium™ - Bone & Stones

The City of Golems

The world has grown cold and heavy. In this second installment of the story-driven saga, you arrive at the gates of a massive metropolis carved from the earth itself. The Kingdom of Stones was once a marvel of architecture, but now, it is a silent tomb of rock.

The City of Golems:

Explore a high-detail, massive map featuring ancient stone architecture, giant bridges, and the ruins of a fallen civilization.

Relentless Bone Hordes:

Face off against waves of skeletons that roam the shadows of the kingdom. Every corner is a test of your survival as the only living presence.

Stone Guardians:

Encounter massive Golems that still follow their ancient commands. These giants of rock are the ultimate test of strength in the Vhalorium universe.

High-Intensity Combat:

Our most action-packed release yet. Engage in fast-paced battles against dragons and bone-hordes, optimized for a smooth experience on both PC and mobile.

Uncover the Great Catastrophe:

Discover the hidden lore of the Kingdom. Why did the stone hearts stop beating? Find the first clues to the mystery that changed reality forever.
